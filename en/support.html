Common questions

The Hijri date is a day off

The Hijri date follows the official moon sighting of the country chosen in Settings, under Moon sighting, or the Umm al-Qura calculation if you choose Calculation only. If your community follows a different date, use Manual adjustment in Settings to add or remove a day.

The Hijri date changes at sunset

The Hijri day traditionally starts at Maghrib. In Settings, "Hijri day starts at" lets you choose sunset or midnight. Sunset needs location access.

My city isn't shown

The app needs location access to find your city. Allow it in your phone's Settings under Privacy & Security, Location Services. The city name also needs an internet connection the first time.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the home screen or lock screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Mizwala.
