Mizwala privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

العربية

Mizwala is a daily calendar that shows the Gregorian and Hijri dates. It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, such as language, digits, colours, when the Hijri day starts, a manual Hijri adjustment and the moon-sighting country.
  • Your last location, rounded to about one kilometre, and the name of your city.

These are kept on your phone only, shared between the app and its widgets. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

The app asks to use your location while you use it. On your phone, the location is used to show your city and, if you choose for the Hijri day to start at sunset, to work out the time of Maghrib.

To name your city, the rounded location is sent to Apple's map service, and only when it has changed. Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy. Your location is never sent to us or included in the statistics below.

You can refuse or turn off location access in your phone's Settings at any time. The calendar keeps working without it; it just can't show your city or start the day at your local sunset.

Anonymous usage statistics

Once a week, the app sends one anonymous summary to Aptabase, a privacy-focused analytics service hosted in the European Union. It contains only:

  • The settings you chose: digits, language, theme colour, appearance, when the Hijri day starts, the manual Hijri adjustment and the page style.
  • Which widget sizes you have added.
  • How many days in the past week you opened the app.
  • The app version, the iOS version and your phone's language.

Aptabase works out the country from the connection. Each summary carries a new random number that is not stored, so summaries cannot be linked to each other or to you. The summary never contains your location, the dates you looked at or any identifier. Widgets send nothing. We use these summaries only to learn which features are used.

What we don't do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share any data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
